- **The task board: projects, main and sub tasks, and your confirmation (#2246).** Fleet › Tasks is now a board.
  It opens on your projects, each with how far along it is, and on what waits for you: finished work to **Confirm** or
  **Send back**, blocked cards to **Answer**. A project is a board of its main tasks by status — drag a card to move
  it, filter by assignee — and each main task opens up its sub tasks. A card opens with every field editable, its
  comments, progress log and history; an edit that started before an instance's newer write is refused rather than
  overwriting it. A main task opened on the dashboard stops for your confirmation when it is finished, and only a
  person signed in on the dashboard confirms it: an instance's `task` `done` on such a card moves it to `review`, and
  the web token alone is held to an instance's rules. Tasks from before keep their ids, under **No project**, and
  retention no longer prunes a project's finished cards. The `task` tool takes a card number (`M-3`) as its `id` and
  lists `review` cards as live. See [features](features.md#on-the-dashboard-fleet--tasks).
