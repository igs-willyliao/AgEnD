- Office: a skin pack's furniture can wait for its person to sit down, and can hide them. An item with `activeWhen` and `activeFor` may set `"activeOn": "seated"`: it turns active only once one of those people, in that state, has arrived at their seat, and turns back as soon as they walk off, so a bed's covers no longer appear before its sleeper gets there (the default, `"state"`, works as before). With `"hides": true`, while the item is active those people are not drawn; their name tags, in one row at the item's tag point, and their speech bubbles are drawn on it, and hovering or clicking it shows their card or opens their chat. Someone walking is always drawn. Together they let a bed, a sedan chair or a tent become one picture with its person, without transparent placeholder pictures. Packs without the keys look the same.
